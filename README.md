# STONE-PAPER-SCISSOR-GAME

## Stone, Paper, Scissors

Stone, Paper, Scissors is a simple two-player game of chance and strategy. Choose one of three hand signs—stone, paper, or scissors—and compare your choice with your opponent’s. The winner is decided by the rules below.

## How to Play

1. Each player chooses stone, paper, or scissors.
2. Both choices are revealed at the same time.
3. Compare the choices to determine the result.

## Rules

- Stone beats scissors.
- Scissors beats paper.
- Paper beats stone.
- If both players choose the same sign, the round is a tie.

## Objective

Win more rounds than your opponent. You can play a single round or agree on a number of rounds before starting.

Have fun!
